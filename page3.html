ABOUT THE MAGAZINE

'Chevy, the magazine' is a fully youth/teen-led online magazine specializing in art, pop culture/fashion, news, and STEM. Our main mission is to make the pitching and publication process for young writers, artists, and scientists as simple and enjoyable as possible (and we would be VERY thankful if you decide to pitch to us!).


If you have questions, email us at chevythemagazine@gmail.com.
